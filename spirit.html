Rock 'n Roll Dream 🎸

The Missoula Children's Theatre presents Sleeping Beauty — an original adaptation of the classic fairy tale. A new princess is born, and all the people of the kingdom gather to celebrate. But one invitation goes missing, and the wrath of the vicious Scarella falls upon them in the form of a terrible spell: Sleeping Beauty is destined to sleep for eternity before she reaches her sixteenth birthday.

The Fairy Godmothers do their very best to soften the curse, but Scarella — with the help of some exceedingly nasty Trolls — tricks Sleeping Beauty into pricking her finger, and the spell takes hold. Five hundred years pass… and Sleeping Beauty wakes to find herself in a Rock 'n Roll dream.

This is definitely no sleeper.

A special thank you

This production came to life through the extraordinary heart and vision of our principal, Dr. Berry — a true champion of the arts at St. Symphorosa. The Missoula Children's Theatre holds a deeply personal place for her: her own children once stood on a stage just like this one, and a lifelong love of the performing arts has been woven through her family for generations. Her mother's devotion to the arts lives on in every child who steps into costume, finds their voice, and discovers what they are truly capable of. It is through Dr. Berry's belief, her dedication, and the quiet generosity of her spirit that our school had this extraordinary week. We are grateful beyond words.

About MCT

For over 50 years, the Missoula Children's Theatre has been bringing the transformative power of live performance to communities across the country — and the world. Their touring company visits more than 1,000 communities each year, casting entirely from local students and mounting a fully staged production in a single week.

What you witnessed last night was the result of just five days of rehearsal. Your children learned lines, songs, choreography, and blocking — and delivered a show that would make any stage proud. That is the MCT magic.
